# Changelog

## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-07

Initial research release: **Certified Structures**.

- A 13-page LaTeX note with explicit hypotheses, full structural proofs, exact board witnesses, and a reproducibility manifest.
- A classification/counting formula for universally legal response matchings after an opening on odd-width, odd-height boards.
- A restricted counterstrategy theorem for nine columns and every odd height at least five.
- Exhaustive 9x9 response-family replay and manuscript audits at heights five and seven.
- Independent midgame pairing certificates, a complete winning endgame strategy tree, and a reachable full-board draw.
- Complementary WDL, finite-horizon minimax, and proof-number search implementations; the recorded exact horizon reaches 27 plies.
- A compatible, reconciled cache of 16,650 normalized position keys, including 11,051 exact values.
- Public verification automation, source/PDF identity checks, and retained upstream licensing.

The opening outcome remains unresolved. This is a reproducible research release, not a solved-game announcement.
